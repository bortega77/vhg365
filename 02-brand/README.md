# 02-brand

The rules of the look. If someone asks what red we use, the answer lives here.

## What to put here

- colors.md with hex codes for every brand color, named.
- fonts/ with the actual font files, plus a note on what is licensed for web.
- Brand guideline PDFs from any agency or OEM.
- Anything that constrains a design rather than being a design.

## Why it matters

Six months from now somebody builds an ad and eyeballs the red. It will be
close but wrong, and every piece after that drifts a little further. Write the
hex codes down once and that never happens.
